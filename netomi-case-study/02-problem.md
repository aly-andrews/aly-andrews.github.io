speaker_notes:
  - So what problem are we solving with this Netomi case study.
  - Weekly shipping with incomplete inputs creates inconsistent release communication.
  - I do not see this as a writing speed problem alone. I see it as a reliability and trust problem.
  - The cost of inconsistent release communication shows up in three places: user confusion, last minute team scramble and ultimately support debt.
  - When release notes and product behavior do not match, users lose trust and contact support more often. Every avoidable support contact costs the company time and money.
  - When documentation is clear and consistent, more users can self serve, which improves support deflection and reduces operational cost.
