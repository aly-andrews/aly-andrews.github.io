<p>I would roll this out in phases so the team can adopt it without disruption. The goal is steady improvement, not a sudden workflow overhaul.</p>

<h3>Phase 0: review and audit current state (weeks 1 to 2)</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Review current release workflow from ticket creation to publish</li>
  <li>Audit what is already structured versus unstructured in docs</li>
  <li>Identify high friction manual steps</li>
  <li>Capture baseline metrics: cycle time, late changes and post release corrections</li>
</ul>

<h3>Phase 1 and 2: lightweight standards then draft automation (weeks 3 to 8)</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Add documentation readiness fields in Jira and align on risk tiers</li>
  <li>Generate weekly first drafts from Jira changes</li>
  <li>Keep full writer review before publish</li>
</ul>

<h3>Phase 3: stabilize and scale (weeks 9 to 12)</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Run short retros, tune templates and expand once quality is stable</li>
</ul>
