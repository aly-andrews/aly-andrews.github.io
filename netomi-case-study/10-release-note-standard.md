<p>This standard keeps weekly release communication clear, consistent and easier to scale.</p>

<h3>Required fields</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Type: New, Improvement, Changed, Action required or Internal</li>
  <li>Title, summary and who is affected</li>
  <li>What action is needed (or no action required)</li>
  <li>Known limitations or rollout notes</li>
  <li>Learn more links</li>
</ul>

<h3>Voice and style rules</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Lead with user impact, then technical detail</li>
  <li>Use plain language and short sentences</li>
  <li>Avoid internal jargon in customer notes</li>
  <li>Be explicit about pending confirmations</li>
</ul>
