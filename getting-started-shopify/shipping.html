Shipping

Shopify supports a variety of shipping carriers, rate types and fulfillment approaches, and the right setup depends entirely on your business. Before configuring your rates, it is worth exploring your options to understand what suits your products, volume and workflow.

On this page

  • Why shipping matters
  • Key shipping concepts
  • Shipping profiles
  • Shipping zones
  • Adding weight-based rates

Where to start

If you sell one or two products and already know exactly what shipping costs with Canada Post, a manual rate where you set a flat or weight-based price and print labels yourself may be all you need.

If you have a large catalogue with items of varying sizes and weights, connecting a carrier like UPS or FedEx and using calculated rates can take the guesswork out of pricing and ensure customers are charged accurately at checkout.

Take some time to explore the shipping settings in Shopify and research your carrier options to find a setup that saves you time and protects your margins.

Why shipping matters

Shipping configuration directly affects whether you can make sales and how much money you actually keep.

Consideration Why it matters
Checkout requirement Customers cannot complete checkout without at least one shipping zone and rate.
Profit margins Shipping costs that are too low can erase your profit on every order.
Where you can sell Shipping zones determine which countries and regions your store is available to.

Key shipping concepts

These terms work together. Zones define where you ship, rates define how much it costs, and profiles let you apply different rules to different products.

Shipping profiles

Shipping profiles let you create different shipping rules for different types of products. Most new merchants should start with only the default shipping profile.

Tip

Begin with the default shipping profile. Add more profiles later only if you sell very different types of products.

Shipping zones

A shipping zone is a group of countries or regions that share the same shipping rates. Start by setting up a domestic shipping zone.

How to create a domestic shipping zone

  1. Go to Settings > Shipping and delivery
  2. In the Shipping zones section, click Add shipping zone (or edit the existing domestic one)
  3. Enter a zone name (for example, "Domestic" or "Canada")
  4. Click Add countries/regions and select your country
  5. Click Done

Adding weight-based rates

Weight-based rates are recommended for most new stores because they reflect actual shipping costs more accurately.

How to add a weight-based shipping rate

  1. Go to Settings > Shipping and delivery
  2. Click on your domestic shipping zone
  3. Under Shipping rates, click Add rate
  4. Enter a rate name (for example, "Standard shipping")
  5. Select Weight-based under Rate type
  6. Set the pricing:
    • First weight (for example, first 1 lb = $8)
    • Additional weight (for example, $2 per extra lb)
  7. Optionally add a handling fee to cover packaging
  8. Click Save

Tip

Always research real shipping costs from your carrier before setting rates. Make sure every product has an accurate weight entered in Shopify.
