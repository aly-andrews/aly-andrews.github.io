Checkout

Checkout is the final step before a customer completes their purchase. How you configure checkout settings has a big impact on conversion rates, meaning the percentage of visitors who actually complete a purchase.

On this page

  • Why checkout matters
  • Checkout customization options
  • Customer contact method
  • Customer information fields

Why checkout matters

Checkout configuration is essential because:

Consideration Why it matters
Checkout is the final step If checkout doesn't work smoothly, customers abandon their carts.
Mobile checkout A large portion of store visitors are on mobile devices. Checkout must work well on phones.
Customer accounts Requiring customers to create accounts can reduce conversions.
Checkout fields Too many required fields can cause customers to abandon their purchase.

Note

Shopify is moving toward One-Page Checkout as the default. This guidance reflects the current single-page checkout flow.

Checkout customization options

Checkout settings let you control how customers complete their purchase. The main areas you can customize are customer accounts, contact method, information fields and order processing.

Accessing checkout settings

To review or change these settings:

  1. In your Shopify admin, go to Settings → Checkout.

This section controls how customers receive order and shipping notifications.

Setting Recommendation for most new stores
Phone number or email Recommended: gives customers more flexibility
Email only Simpler but less flexible
Show a link for customers to track their order with Shop Keep enabled: improves customer experience
Require customers to sign in before checkout Usually disabled: requiring accounts reduces conversions

Customer information fields

These settings control what information customers are asked to provide during checkout.

Field Recommendation
Full name Keep required (first and last name)
Company name Don't include (recommended for most stores)
Address line 2 (apartment, unit, etc.) Optional (good default)
Shipping address phone number Don't include (unless needed for delivery)
