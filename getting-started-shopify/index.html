Overview

This manual gives new Shopify merchants clear instructions for completing the essential setup work before selling online. It focuses on five areas to configure for a store to accept and fulfill orders: store settings, payments, products, shipping and checkout.

Use this manual together with the Shopify Help Center for detailed screenshots and country specific rules.

What is Shopify?

Shopify is an ecommerce platform for building and managing an online store. One admin handles products, payments, shipping and checkout.

Shopify does not market the business or generate sales. Each business is required to set up the store, add products and run their own marketing.

Note

This manual is not affiliated with or endorsed by Shopify Inc. Product names, trademarks and registered trademarks belong to their respective owners.

Who should read this manual

This manual is for people who:

  • Have created a Shopify store but have not launched it yet
  • Have limited experience with the Shopify admin
  • Need simple, practical instructions to get a basic store ready to accept orders

How to use this manual

  1. Start with the essential setup overview. Use it as a quick map of the five sections.
  2. Open your Shopify admin. Follow each procedure while you are logged in, one step at a time.
  3. Move through sections in order. Each section builds on the previous one.
  4. Use the reference materials. The troubleshooting guide, glossary and FAQs can help when you get stuck.
