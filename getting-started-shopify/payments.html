Payments

A payment gateway is a service that allows you to accept payments online. It is critical for your store to function. Without a configured payment provider, customers cannot complete checkout and you cannot process orders.

Setting up payment processing early helps you understand costs and ensures your store can accept orders when you're ready to launch.

Why payment setup matters

Payment gateway configuration is essential because:

Consideration Details
Checkout Requirement Customers cannot complete checkout without a payment provider. Your store cannot accept orders until payment is configured, even on a free trial.
Provider Availability Payment provider availability depends on your location and what you sell. Not all providers are available in all regions, and some restrict certain products.
Impact on Pricing Understanding fees helps you price products correctly. Payment fees directly affect your profit margins.
Business Verification Some providers require business verification. Setup can take time, so starting early prevents delays.

Payment provider options

There are two main types of payment providers: Shopify Payments and third party providers.

Shopify Payments is Shopify's built-in card processing in the admin. When it is available in your region and approved for your business, you usually pay processing fees only and you do not pay Shopify's separate transaction fee on each order.

A third party provider (for example PayPal or Stripe) connects as an external gateway. Checkout still happens in your store but that provider moves the money and sets its own rates and rules. Shopify often adds a transaction fee when you use a third party instead of Shopify Payments.

Warning

Some providers (including Shopify Payments) may reject certain high risk products such as CBD, some supplements or adult content. Research provider requirements before you invest heavily in your store setup.

Understanding Payment Fees

New merchants often confuse two types of fees. Here's a clear breakdown:

Transaction Fees Credit Card Processing Fees
Who charges it? Shopify (when using a third-party provider) The payment processor (Shopify Payments or third-party like PayPal)
When is it charged? Only when using third-party payment providers On every transaction, regardless of provider
Typical Cost Usually 0.5% – 2% (varies by Shopify plan) e.g. 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction
Charged by Shopify Payments? No Yes
Example You pay extra to Shopify if using PayPal You always pay this to the actual processor

Summary example:

Using Shopify Payments: You only pay Credit Card Processing Fees (e.g., 2.9% + $0.30)

Using PayPal: You pay both Transaction Fees (to Shopify) + Credit Card Processing Fees (to PayPal)

Tip

Factor payment fees into your product pricing. If you're paying 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction, make sure your profit margins account for these costs.
