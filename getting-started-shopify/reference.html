Troubleshooting guide

Use this table to quickly solve common setup problems. For more complex issues, refer to the Shopify Help Center or contact Shopify Support.

Area Issue Symptoms Quick fix
Store settings Address or legal pages show old details You updated store address but policies are outdated Update policy pages manually in Online Store → Pages
Payments No payment options at checkout Customers cannot select a payment method Activate at least one payment provider and verify your account
Products Products not showing on storefront Storefront looks empty even after adding products Make products Active and assign them to the Online Store sales channel
Shipping No shipping options at checkout Error or stuck at shipping step Create at least one shipping zone and rate
Checkout Checkout feels too long High cart abandonment at checkout Reduce required fields and keep guest checkout enabled

Glossary

Key terms used throughout this manual:

Admin (Shopify Admin)
The backend dashboard where you manage products, orders, customers, and settings.
Online Store
Your public website where customers browse and buy products.
Sales Channel
A place where you sell products (Online Store, Facebook, Instagram, POS, etc.).
Collection
A group of products displayed together (e.g. “New Arrivals” or “Sale”).
Product Variant
Different versions of the same product (e.g. different sizes or colors).
Shipping Zone
A geographic area you ship to (country or region).
Shipping Profile
A set of shipping rules that can be applied to specific products.
Payment Gateway / Provider
The service that processes customer payments (Shopify Payments, PayPal, Stripe, etc.).
Checkout
The final process where customers enter their information and complete their order.

Frequently asked questions (FAQs)

General questions

Q: How long does it take to set up a Shopify store?
A: Completing the five essential setup areas typically takes 2–4 hours, depending on how many products you're adding and how complex your shipping needs are. You can complete the setup over multiple sessions. You don't need to do everything at once.

Q: Do I need technical skills to use Shopify?
A: No. Shopify is designed for beginners. This manual assumes no prior technical experience. If you can use a web browser and fill out forms, you can set up a Shopify store.

Q: Can I change settings after launching?
A: Yes. Most settings can be changed after launch. However, some changes (like store address or currency) can affect other areas such as legal pages, shipping and taxes. It's easier to get settings right before launch, but you can update them later if needed.

Payments

Q: What if Shopify Payments isn't available in my country?
A: You'll need to use an alternative payment provider such as PayPal or Stripe. This means you'll pay transaction fees in addition to credit card processing fees. Budget accordingly and factor these fees into your product pricing.

Q: What's the difference between transaction fees and credit card processing fees?
A: Transaction fees are charged by Shopify when you use a third-party payment provider (not Shopify Payments). Credit card processing fees are charged by payment processors, and everyone pays these. If you use Shopify Payments, you only pay credit card processing fees with no transaction fees.

Shipping

Q: Do I have to ship worldwide?
A: No. You can configure shipping zones to ship only to specific countries or regions. Start with domestic shipping, then add international zones if desired.

Q: What's the difference between shipping zones and shipping profiles?
A: Shipping zones define where you ship (countries and regions). Shipping profiles define different shipping rules for different products. Most merchants start with one shipping profile (the default) and multiple zones.

Checkout

Q: Should I require customers to create accounts?
A: Generally no. Requiring accounts can reduce conversion rates. Allow guest checkout, but offer account creation as an option. Customers can create accounts after purchase if desired.

Q: How many checkout fields should I require?
A: As few as possible. Only require fields you absolutely need (email, shipping address, payment). Every additional required field increases the chance of cart abandonment.
